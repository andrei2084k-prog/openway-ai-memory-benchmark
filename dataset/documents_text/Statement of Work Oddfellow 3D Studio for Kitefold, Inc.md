# Statement of Work: Oddfellow 3D Studio for Kitefold, Inc.

1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 This Statement of Work describes the 3D modeling services Oddfellow will provide to Kitefold for use in WebAR experiences built on the Kitefold platform. Oddfellow will create web-ready 3D models from reference material that Kitefold supplies, such as product photographs, dimensioned drawings, CAD exports or scan data.

1.2 Work is ordered model by model. Each model is assigned to one of three tiers (S, M or L), described in Section 2. The tier is confirmed in writing by both parties before work on that model begins.

2. Model Tiers

2.1 Tier S (simple). Suited to single-part objects with clean shapes, such as a bottle, a mug, a box or a simple piece of signage. Tier S models are built to a low polygon budget, use one material, and ship with one texture set (base color only). Surface detail is kept to what reads well on a phone screen at arm's length.

2.2 Tier M (standard). Suited to products with several parts or moderate surface complexity, such as a sneaker, a small appliance or a piece of furniture. Tier M models are built to a medium polygon budget with up to 3 materials. They ship with up to 2 texture sets, covering base color, roughness, metalness and normal detail.

2.3 Tier L (complex). Suited to detailed hero objects, assemblies with many parts, or items with fine geometry and mixed materials. Tier L models are built to a high polygon budget, with detail baked from a higher-resolution source model where needed. They ship with up to 4 texture sets, including emissive or transparency maps where the object calls for them, and with a lower-detail version for slower devices.

2.4 Every model, at any tier, is optimized for mobile browsers: clean topology, correct real-world scale, a sensible origin point, and no hidden or unused geometry. Exact polygon and texture limits for each tier are set out in the technical specification sheet that Oddfellow will send Marta-to-Kitefold at kickoff and that forms part of this Statement of Work.

2.5 If, after reviewing the reference material, Oddfellow believes a model belongs in a different tier from the one first proposed, Oddfellow will tell Kitefold before starting work. Kitefold may approve the change, adjust the brief, or cancel that model at no charge.

3. Prices

3.1 Prices are per model, according to tier:

Tier S: $180 per model

Tier M: $420 per model

Tier L: $950 per model

3.2 The price for a model covers modeling, texturing, optimization, the revision rounds described in Section 5, and delivery of the files listed in Section 6. No separate charge applies for project management or file preparation.

3.3 All prices are in US dollars and exclude any applicable sales tax.

4. Turnaround

4.1 Standard turnaround, counted in business days from the day Oddfellow confirms it has received a complete brief and all reference material for the model, is:

Tier S: 5 business days

Tier M: 8 business days

Tier L: 12 business days

4.2 Turnaround means delivery of the first complete version of the model for Kitefold's review. If the reference material is incomplete, Oddfellow will say so within 1 business day of receipt, and the clock starts when the missing items arrive.

4.3 Models ordered together are scheduled in the order Kitefold ranks them. Oddfellow will give a combined schedule at kickoff for any batch of more than one model.

5. Revisions

5.1 Each model includes 2 revision rounds. A revision round is one consolidated set of change requests from Kitefold, sent in a single message, relating to the approved brief.

5.2 Changes that fall outside the original brief, such as a new colorway, a different object, added parts or a change of tier, are not revisions. Oddfellow will quote them separately in writing, and no work on them starts until Kitefold approves the quote.

5.3 Oddfellow will return each revision round within 2 business days for Tier S and within 3 business days for Tiers M and L, counted from receipt of the consolidated requests.

5.4 Revision rounds beyond the 2 included are available on request. Oddfellow will quote them in writing before doing the work.

6. File Formats and Delivery

6.1 Oddfellow will deliver each model as a binary glTF (.glb) file, which is the primary format for use in the Kitefold editor. On request, Oddfellow will also provide a USDZ file for the same model at no extra charge.

6.2 Texture maps will be embedded in the .glb file. On request, Oddfellow will also supply the maps as separate image files.

6.3 Oddfellow will provide the editable source scene on request, so that Kitefold or its other contractors can make later adjustments.

6.4 Files are delivered through a private download link sent to Kitefold's named contact. Links remain active until Kitefold confirms receipt.

7. Acceptance

7.1 Kitefold will review each delivered model and either accept it or send revision requests within 5 business days of delivery.

7.2 A model is accepted when Kitefold confirms acceptance in writing. If Kitefold does not respond within the review period, Oddfellow will send one reminder. If there is still no response 2 business days after the reminder, the model is treated as accepted.

7.3 Acceptance means the model matches the approved brief and the tier specification. It does not limit Oddfellow's obligation to correct a defect that makes the file fail to load or display as delivered.

8. Invoicing and Payment

8.1 Oddfellow will invoice each model once it is accepted. Invoices list the model name, its tier and the price from Section 3.

8.2 Payment is due 30 days from the invoice date. Invoices are sent by email to the billing contact Kitefold names at kickoff.

8.3 If Kitefold cancels a model after work has started but before delivery, Oddfellow may invoice for the work completed to that point, not exceeding the price of the tier.

9. Rights and Confidentiality

9.1 On payment of the invoice for a model, all rights in the delivered model and its textures transfer to Kitefold, excluding Oddfellow's general tools, techniques and pre-existing assets.

9.2 Oddfellow will treat all reference material, unreleased products and brand assets that Kitefold shares as confidential and will use them only for the work described here. Oddfellow may not show or describe Kitefold's work publicly without Kitefold's written permission.

10. Term

10.1 This Statement of Work takes effect when both parties have signed it and continues until either party ends it with 30 days' written notice. Models already accepted or in progress when notice is given will be completed and paid under these terms.
