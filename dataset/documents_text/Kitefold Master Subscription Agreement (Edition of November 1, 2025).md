# Kitefold Master Subscription Agreement (Edition of November 1, 2025)

KITEFOLD MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

Edition of November 1, 2025

This Master Subscription Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into between Kitefold, Inc., whose office is at 418 Tidewater Avenue, Suite 5, Port Calloway ("Kitefold"), and the customer identified in the applicable Order Form (the "Customer"). This edition is dated November 1, 2025. Kitefold and the Customer are each a "party" and together the "parties". By signing an Order Form that refers to this Agreement, or by accessing the Platform, the Customer agrees to be bound by it.

1. Definitions

1.1 "Platform" means the no-code, web-based augmented reality creation, hosting and delivery service operated by Kitefold, including the editor, the viewer, the publishing tools, the analytics dashboard and all related documentation, as made available to the Customer from time to time.

1.2 "Order Form" means a document signed or accepted online by both parties that identifies the Customer, the subscription plan, the Subscription Term, the monthly View quota, the Fees and the overage rate, and that incorporates this Agreement by reference.

1.3 "Experience" means a web-based interactive experience created with the Platform and published by or for the Customer.

1.4 "View" has the meaning given in Section 5.1.

1.5 "Customer Content" means all models, images, video, audio, text, marker images, brand assets, data and other materials that the Customer or its authorized users upload to, create on or publish through the Platform, but excluding the Platform itself and Kitefold's Materials.

1.6 "Kitefold Materials" means the Platform, its underlying software, templates, viewers, libraries, tracking and rendering technology, documentation, and all improvements, modifications and derivative works of any of them, together with Kitefold's trademarks and trade names.

1.7 "Authorized User" means an employee, contractor or agent of the Customer whom the Customer permits to use the Platform under its account and who has been issued credentials for that purpose.

1.8 "Subscription Term" means the initial term stated in the Order Form together with each renewal term under Section 3.

1.9 "Fees" means the subscription fees, overage charges and any other amounts payable by the Customer under an Order Form.

1.10 "Documentation" means the user guides, help articles and technical specifications that Kitefold publishes for the Platform, as updated from time to time.

2. Subscription and access

2.1 Grant of access. Subject to this Agreement and the applicable Order Form, Kitefold grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable right during the Subscription Term to access and use the Platform, through its Authorized Users, to create, publish and operate Experiences for the Customer's own business purposes or, where the Order Form so provides, for the Customer's own customers.

2.2 Plans. The features, limits and support level available to the Customer are those of the plan stated in the Order Form. Kitefold may improve or modify the Platform from time to time, provided that it does not materially reduce the core functionality of the plan for which the Customer has paid during the Subscription Term.

2.3 Credentials. The Customer is responsible for all activity under its account and for keeping credentials confidential. The Customer shall notify Kitefold promptly upon becoming aware of any unauthorized use of its account.

2.4 Support. Kitefold shall provide support for the Platform through its support desk in accordance with the support level stated in the Order Form and the Documentation.

2.5 Reservation of rights. Kitefold and its licensors retain all right, title and interest in the Kitefold Materials. No rights are granted to the Customer except as expressly stated in this Agreement. The Customer shall not copy, reverse engineer or attempt to extract the source code of the Platform, except to the extent applicable law forbids this restriction.

2.6 Feedback. If the Customer gives Kitefold suggestions or feedback about the Platform, Kitefold may use them without restriction or obligation to the Customer.

3. Term and renewal

3.1 Term. This Agreement begins when the Customer signs or accepts the first Order Form and continues for as long as any Order Form remains in effect. Each Order Form states the initial Subscription Term.

3.2 Automatic renewal. Subscriptions renew automatically for 12 months unless either side gives notice at least 60 days before the renewal date. The renewal date is the day on which the then-current Subscription Term ends. Notice of non-renewal must be given in writing in accordance with Section 14.

3.3 Terms of renewal. Each renewal term is subject to this Agreement and to the Order Form then in effect, with the Fees determined in accordance with Section 7.

3.4 Termination for cause. Either party may terminate this Agreement or an Order Form by written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within a reasonable period after receiving written notice describing the breach. Kitefold may also suspend access under Section 8.3.

3.5 Effect of termination or expiry. When a subscription ends, the Customer's right to access the Platform ends, and Experiences published under that subscription may cease to be delivered. Customer Content is handled as set out in Section 9. Fees that have accrued before the end of the subscription remain payable. Fees paid in advance are not refundable, except where the Customer terminates for Kitefold's uncured material breach, in which case Kitefold shall refund the prepaid Fees attributable to the remainder of the Subscription Term.

3.6 Survival. Sections 1, 4 (as to amounts accrued), 6.3, 9, 10.3, 11, 12 and 14, and any other provisions that by their nature are intended to survive, survive the end of this Agreement.

4. Fees and payment

4.1 Fees. The Customer shall pay the Fees stated in the Order Form. Unless the Order Form states otherwise, Fees are stated and payable in United States dollars and are exclusive of sales, use, value-added and similar taxes, which the Customer shall bear, other than taxes on Kitefold's net income.

4.2 Invoicing and payment terms. Invoices are payable Net 30; late amounts carry 1.5% interest a month. Interest accrues from the due date until the amount is paid in full, or, if lower, at the maximum rate permitted by applicable law.

4.3 Disputed amounts. If the Customer disputes an invoice in good faith, it shall notify Kitefold in writing before the due date, giving reasonable detail, and shall pay the undisputed portion on time. The parties shall work together promptly to resolve the dispute.

4.4 Suspension for non-payment. If an undisputed amount remains unpaid after its due date and Kitefold has given the Customer written notice of the delinquency, Kitefold may suspend access to the Platform until the amount is paid. Suspension does not relieve the Customer of its obligation to pay Fees for the suspension period.

4.5 No set-off. The Customer shall pay all amounts due without set-off or deduction, except for amounts disputed under Section 4.3.

4.6 Non-cancellable. Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, Fees are non-cancellable and the obligation to pay them continues for the full Subscription Term.

5. Views, quotas and overage

5.1 Definition of a View. A view is one load of an experience on one device; reloads on the same device within 30 minutes count once. Kitefold measures Views using the Platform's own delivery and analytics systems, and those measurements are the basis for quota and overage calculations absent manifest error.

5.2 Monthly quota. Each Order Form states a monthly View quota. The quota applies to the aggregate of all Experiences published under the Customer's subscription in each calendar month. Unused Views do not carry over to a later month and are not refundable.

5.3 Overage. Views over the monthly quota are billed monthly in arrears at the overage rate in the order form. Overage charges are invoiced after the end of the month in which the excess Views occurred and are payable under Section 4.2.

5.4 Continued delivery. Kitefold will not ordinarily interrupt delivery of Experiences merely because the monthly quota has been exceeded. Kitefold may, however, act under Section 8.3 if traffic is generated artificially or in a manner that threatens the stability of the Platform.

5.5 Reporting. The Customer may review its Views in the analytics dashboard. Kitefold shall, on reasonable written request, explain how a particular month's figures were calculated.

5.6 Plan changes. The Customer may ask to move to a plan with a larger quota at any time by agreeing a new or amended Order Form with Kitefold. A change takes effect only when both parties have agreed it in writing.

6. Customer content

6.1 Ownership. As between the parties, the Customer owns all right, title and interest in the Customer Content. Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of Customer Content to Kitefold.

6.2 License to Kitefold. The Customer grants Kitefold a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license during the Subscription Term, and for the period described in Section 9, to host, store, reproduce, process, transmit and display the Customer Content solely as needed to provide, secure, support and improve the Platform for the Customer, and to deliver the Customer's Experiences to their audiences.

6.3 Customer responsibilities. The Customer is solely responsible for the Customer Content and represents and warrants that it has, and will maintain, all rights, licenses, consents and permissions necessary to upload and publish the Customer Content and to grant the license in Section 6.2, including rights in any third-party models, images, music, trademarks and likenesses.

6.4 Removal. Kitefold may remove or disable access to any Customer Content or Experience that it reasonably believes violates Section 8 or applicable law. Where practicable, Kitefold shall notify the Customer of the removal and the reason for it.

6.5 Aggregated data. Kitefold may collect and use technical and usage data about the operation of the Platform in aggregated or de-identified form to maintain, secure and improve the Platform, provided that the data does not identify the Customer or any individual.

7. Price changes

7.1 Fixed pricing during a term. The Fees stated in an Order Form are fixed for the Subscription Term then in effect.

7.2 Renewal pricing. New list prices apply to an existing subscription only at its renewal, with 45 days' written notice. Kitefold shall give that notice in accordance with Section 14, and the notice shall identify the new list prices that will apply to the renewal term.

7.3 No mid-term change. Kitefold shall not apply new list prices, additional charges or a revised overage rate to an existing subscription before its renewal, except as agreed in a new or amended Order Form signed by both parties.

7.4 Effect of notice. If the Customer does not give notice of non-renewal under Section 3.2, the subscription renews on the terms in Section 3.2 and at the prices notified under Section 7.2.

8. Acceptable use

8.1 Obligations. The Customer shall use the Platform only in accordance with this Agreement, the Documentation and applicable law. The Customer shall ensure that its Authorized Users do the same.

8.2 Prohibited conduct. The Customer shall not, and shall not permit anyone else to: (a) upload or publish content that is unlawful, defamatory, harassing, infringing, deceptive or otherwise objectionable; (b) publish an Experience that collects personal information from its audience without the notices and consents the law requires; (c) interfere with or disrupt the integrity or performance of the Platform or the data contained in it; (d) attempt to gain unauthorized access to the Platform or its related systems; (e) generate artificial Views by automated or fraudulent means; (f) use the Platform to build a competing product or service; or (g) resell or sublicense access to the Platform except as the Order Form expressly permits.

8.3 Suspension. If Kitefold reasonably believes that the Customer or an Authorized User has violated this Section 8, or that use of the Platform poses a security risk or a risk of harm to Kitefold or others, Kitefold may suspend the relevant access or Experience. Kitefold shall limit the suspension to what is reasonably necessary and shall restore access promptly once the cause has been resolved.

8.4 Camera and device permissions. Experiences may request access to a device's camera or sensors. The Customer shall ensure that each Experience explains clearly to the audience why access is requested and shall not use camera or sensor data for any purpose the audience has not been told about.

9. Data and deletion

9.1 Security. Kitefold shall maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards that are designed to protect Customer Content against unauthorized access, loss or alteration and that are appropriate to the nature of the Platform.

9.2 Export. During the Subscription Term, the Customer may export its Customer Content using the tools the Platform provides. The Customer is responsible for retaining its own copies of Customer Content that it wishes to keep.

9.3 Deletion. Customer content is deleted 90 days after the subscription ends. Until then, Kitefold shall hold the Customer Content securely and, on written request, shall make it available for export, but is not required to continue delivering published Experiences.

9.4 Backups. Copies of Customer Content held in routine backups are overwritten in the ordinary course of Kitefold's backup cycle following deletion, and remain subject to the confidentiality and security commitments of this Agreement until overwritten.

9.5 Personal data. To the extent Kitefold processes personal data on the Customer's behalf, it shall do so only on the Customer's documented instructions and as needed to provide the Platform. If applicable law requires a separate data processing addendum, the parties shall enter into one on reasonable terms.

9.6 Confidentiality. Each party shall keep confidential the non-public information of the other party that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, shall use it only to perform or exercise rights under this Agreement, and shall disclose it only to personnel and advisers who need to know it and who are bound by comparable obligations. These obligations do not apply to information that is public through no fault of the recipient, was lawfully known to or independently developed by the recipient, or must be disclosed by law, in which case the recipient shall, where lawful, give the other party prior notice.

10. Warranties

10.1 Mutual. Each party represents that it has the authority to enter into this Agreement and that doing so does not breach any other agreement by which it is bound.

10.2 Kitefold. Kitefold warrants that it will provide the Platform with reasonable skill and care and in substantial conformity with the Documentation. If the Platform does not conform, the Customer's exclusive remedy, and Kitefold's sole obligation, is for Kitefold to use reasonable efforts to correct the non-conformity or, if it cannot do so within a reasonable time, to permit the Customer to terminate the affected Order Form and receive a refund of prepaid Fees for the remainder of the Subscription Term.

10.3 Customer. The Customer warrants that the Customer Content and its use under this Agreement will not infringe or misappropriate the rights of any third party or violate applicable law. The Customer shall defend and indemnify Kitefold against third-party claims arising from a breach of this warranty or of Section 8, provided that Kitefold gives prompt notice of the claim, allows the Customer to control the defense and settlement (except that no settlement may impose liability or admissions on Kitefold without its consent), and gives reasonable cooperation at the Customer's expense.

10.4 Kitefold indemnity. Kitefold shall defend the Customer against third-party claims alleging that the Platform, as provided by Kitefold and used in accordance with this Agreement, infringes that third party's intellectual property rights, and shall pay the damages and costs finally awarded or agreed in settlement, subject to the same conditions as in Section 10.3 and to Section 11. Kitefold has no obligation for claims arising from Customer Content, from combinations with items not supplied by Kitefold, or from use in breach of this Agreement.

10.5 Disclaimer. Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, the Platform is provided "as is" and Kitefold disclaims all other warranties, express or implied, including implied warranties of merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose and non-infringement. Kitefold does not warrant that the Platform will be uninterrupted or error-free, or that every device, browser or camera will support every Experience.

11. Limitation of liability

11.1 Cap. Each party's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement and all Order Forms, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, is capped at the fees paid in the 12 months before the claim.

11.2 Exclusion of indirect loss. Neither party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for loss of profits, revenue, goodwill or data, however caused, even if advised of the possibility of such loss.

11.3 Exceptions. The limitations in this Section 11 do not apply to: (a) the Customer's obligation to pay Fees; (b) liability that cannot be limited under applicable law, including liability for fraud or willful misconduct; or (c) a party's breach of its confidentiality obligations under Section 9.6, to the extent applicable law permits that exception.

11.4 Basis of the bargain. The parties acknowledge that the Fees reflect the allocation of risk in this Section 11 and that each party has relied on these limitations in entering into this Agreement.

12. Governing law and venue

12.1 Governing law. This Agreement, and any dispute arising out of or relating to it, is governed by the laws of the jurisdiction in which Port Calloway County is situated, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules.

12.2 Venue. Disputes go to the courts of Port Calloway County. Each party submits to the jurisdiction of those courts and waives any objection to venue in them, including any objection based on inconvenient forum.

12.3 Informal resolution. Before starting proceedings, each party shall give the other written notice of the dispute and the parties shall make a good-faith attempt to resolve it informally. This does not prevent either party from seeking urgent injunctive relief to protect its intellectual property or confidential information.

12.4 Waiver of jury trial. To the extent permitted by applicable law, each party waives any right to a trial by jury in any proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

13. Publicity

13.1 Reference use. Kitefold may show the customer's logo as a reference unless the customer opts out in writing. Kitefold may display the logo on its website, in presentations and in customer lists, in each case without implying endorsement beyond the fact that the Customer uses the Platform.

13.2 Opting out. The Customer may opt out at any time by giving written notice under Section 14. After receiving an opt-out notice, Kitefold shall stop new uses of the logo within a reasonable time and shall remove it from materials under its control, but is not required to recall materials already distributed.

13.3 Other publicity. Except as stated in Section 13.1, neither party shall issue a press release or publish a case study naming the other party without the other party's prior written approval.

13.4 Trademark guidelines. Any permitted use of a party's name or logo must follow the reasonable brand guidelines that the party has provided. All goodwill from the use of a party's marks accrues to that party.

14. Notices

14.1 Form. Notices under this Agreement, including notices of non-renewal, price notices, opt-out notices and notices of breach, must be in writing and delivered by hand, by a recognized courier, or by email to the address stated in the Order Form.

14.2 Notices to Kitefold. Notices to Kitefold must be addressed to Kitefold, Inc., 418 Tidewater Avenue, Suite 5, Port Calloway, marked for the attention of the Legal Department, with a copy by email to the address that Kitefold designates for notices in the Order Form.

14.3 Notices to the Customer. Notices to the Customer must be sent to the postal and email addresses stated in the Order Form, or to the account owner's email address on the Platform, and may also be given through a prominent message in the Platform where this Agreement allows.

14.4 Receipt. A notice delivered by hand or courier is effective on delivery. A notice sent by email is effective when sent, provided that the sender does not receive an automated message that delivery failed. Notices that arrive outside ordinary business hours are treated as received at the start of the next business day.

14.5 Changes of address. Each party shall notify the other in writing of any change to its notice details. Until it does so, notices sent to the last address given are valid.

14.6 Entire agreement. This Agreement and each Order Form are the entire agreement between the parties on their subject matter and supersede all prior discussions and understandings. If an Order Form conflicts with this Agreement, the Order Form governs only as to the commercial terms it states and only if it expressly identifies the term it overrides. Any amendment must be in writing and signed or accepted by both parties. A failure to enforce a provision is not a waiver of it, and if any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent, except to a successor in a merger or sale of substantially all of its business or assets.
