# Kitefold Service Level Policy (effective July 1, 2026)

Kitefold Service Level Policy

Issued June 15, 2026 by Kitefold, Inc., Port Calloway. This policy takes effect on July 1, 2026 and replaces the November 2025 Service Level Policy.

1. Purpose and scope

1.1 This policy describes what Kitefold commits to for availability, incident response and planned maintenance of the Kitefold platform, and what we do when we fall short. The platform includes the Kitefold editor, the publishing service and the delivery of published WebAR experiences to your audience.

1.2 The policy applies to customers on the Kite, Studio, Stage and Arena plans. Service credits (section 4) apply to Stage and Arena subscriptions. The incident response commitments in section 6 apply as described there. Where a signed order form or master agreement contains different terms, the signed document takes precedence over this policy.

1.3 This policy is part of how we run the service and is meant to be read together with your plan description and our terms of service. It is written to be read by the people who actually depend on the service, not only by lawyers, so we have kept the language plain.

2. Definitions

2.1 Availability means the share of a calendar month in which published experiences can be loaded by your audience and the Kitefold editor and publishing service can be used by your team, expressed as a percentage. Delivery of published experiences relies on our content delivery network, provided by Swiftline Edge, and an outage there that stops experiences from loading counts as unavailability.

2.2 Incident means an unplanned event that degrades or interrupts the platform.

2.3 P1 incident means an incident that makes published experiences unavailable to your audience, or prevents your team from publishing or editing at all, with no reasonable workaround.

2.4 First response means a message from a named Kitefold person confirming that the incident is acknowledged and being worked on. It is not the same as resolution.

2.5 Planned maintenance means work announced in advance and carried out inside the maintenance window defined in section 7.

2.6 Monthly fee means the subscription fee you pay for the plan for the calendar month in which the incident occurred.

3. Availability commitment

3.1 We design, monitor and operate the platform to keep it available at all times. Our infrastructure, deployment and monitoring are run by our DevOps team, who watch the service continuously and respond to alerts when they fire.

3.2 Our availability targets are tied to the credit thresholds in section 4: 99.7% for Stage and 99.9% for Arena. If availability in a calendar month falls below the threshold for your plan, you are entitled to a service credit.

3.3 We calculate availability from our own monitoring records, cross-checked against reports from the delivery network and from customers. When you submit a claim, we will share the relevant monitoring data for the incident you are claiming for.

4. Service credits

4.1 Service credits are the remedy for missing the availability thresholds. Each credit is a percentage of the monthly fee for the affected calendar month and is applied to a future invoice. Credits are not paid out in cash.

4.2 From July 1, 2026, the following credits apply.

4.3 Stage plan: if availability falls below 99.7%, you receive a credit of 5%.

4.4 Arena plan: if availability falls below 99.9%, you receive a credit of 10%. If availability falls below 99.5%, you receive a credit of 25%. If availability falls below 99.0%, you receive a credit of 50%.

4.5 Only the highest applicable credit is given for a calendar month. Credits for different thresholds are not added together.

4.6 Kite and Studio plans do not carry service credits. We still treat every incident on those plans seriously, and the status page and support channels described below remain available to you.

4.7 Service credits are the sole financial remedy for missed availability under this policy. They do not limit your right to end your subscription as set out in your agreement.

5. How to claim a credit

5.1 To receive a credit, you must submit a claim within 45 days of the incident. This claim window applies to incidents from July 1, 2026. Claims for incidents before that date follow the November 2025 policy.

5.2 Send your claim to the Kitefold Care desk. Your claim should include your account name, the plan, the date and approximate time of the incident, the experiences or features affected and any evidence you have, such as screenshots or error messages.

5.3 The Kitefold Care desk will acknowledge your claim and review it against our monitoring records. For Arena accounts, your Customer Success Manager will be copied on the reply.

5.4 If we confirm that availability fell below the threshold for your plan, we will tell you which credit applies and show it on your next invoice. If we disagree with your claim, we will explain why and show the data we relied on.

5.5 Claims submitted after the 45-day window are not eligible for a credit. We may still, at our discretion, respond to the underlying issue.

6. Support and incident response

6.1 Incidents are classified by Kitefold on the basis of their impact on your audience and your team. If you believe an incident has been classified too low, tell the Kitefold Care desk and we will review it promptly.

6.2 For Arena plan customers, we commit to a first response to an Arena P1 incident within 2 hours. This commitment applies from July 1, 2026. It runs from the time you report the incident to the Kitefold Care desk or the time our monitoring detects it, whichever is earlier.

6.3 For all other incidents and plans, first response targets are those described in your plan's support terms. Nothing in this policy shortens or lengthens them.

6.4 During a P1 incident we will post updates on our status page and send them to the account contacts on file. Updates say what is affected, what we know, what we are doing and when we will update you next. We keep updating until the incident is resolved, even when there is no new information.

6.5 After a P1 incident on a Stage or Arena plan, we will provide a written summary on request describing the cause, the impact and the steps we are taking to prevent a repeat.

6.6 Published experiences already live on the delivery network are served independently of the editor in many cases, so an editor incident does not always affect your audience. Our status updates will say which components are affected.

7. Planned maintenance

7.1 From July 1, 2026, we carry out planned maintenance on Sundays 04:00-06:00 Pacific. We do not schedule routine planned maintenance outside this window.

7.2 We will notify account contacts and post on the status page before planned maintenance that may affect the service. The notice describes what will be done and what you might notice.

7.3 Time spent in planned maintenance inside the window is not counted as unavailability for the purposes of section 3 and section 4.

7.4 If maintenance runs beyond the window and the service is unavailable to your audience after 06:00 Pacific, the time beyond the window counts as unavailability.

7.5 Urgent security fixes may occasionally need to be applied outside the window. We will tell you as early as we can, and any resulting unavailability outside the window counts toward availability.

8. Exclusions

8.1 The following are not counted as unavailability and do not qualify for a credit:

(a) planned maintenance carried out inside the window in section 7;

(b) problems caused by your own content, code, custom domains or network configuration;

(c) problems caused by a viewer's device, browser, camera permissions or connection;

(d) suspension or restriction of your account in line with our terms of service;

(e) events outside our reasonable control, such as widespread internet failures, acts of government or natural disasters, except to the extent our own redundancy should have prevented the impact;

(f) features that we have marked as beta, preview or deprecated in the product or its release notes.

9. Reviewing this policy

9.1 We review this policy when our platform or plans change in a way that affects it. Changes are published with a new effective date, and we notify account contacts before they take effect.

9.2 This version was prepared by Kitefold management. Questions about it can be sent to the Kitefold Care desk, or, for Arena customers, to your Customer Success Manager.
