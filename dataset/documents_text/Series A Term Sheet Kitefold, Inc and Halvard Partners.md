# Series A Term Sheet: Kitefold, Inc. and Halvard Partners

SUMMARY OF TERMS FOR THE PROPOSED SERIES A PREFERRED STOCK FINANCING OF KITEFOLD, INC.

Dated: March 27, 2026

Prepared by: Halvard Partners ("Halvard" or the "Lead Investor")

Addressed to: Maya Lindqvist, Founder and Chief Executive Officer, Kitefold, Inc. ("Kitefold" or the "Company")

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms on which Halvard proposes to lead a Series A financing of Kitefold. Except for the sections identified in Part 5 as binding, it is a non-binding expression of intent. No party is obligated to complete the financing unless and until definitive agreements are signed and delivered.

PART 1. THE OFFERING

1.1 Issuer. Kitefold, Inc., the developer of a no-code WebAR platform based in Port Calloway.

1.2 Round size and lead. Series A financing of $7.5 million led by Halvard Partners at a $28 million pre-money valuation. Halvard may syndicate a portion of the round to co-investors acceptable to the Company, and any such co-investor will invest on the same terms as Halvard.

1.3 Security. Series A Preferred Stock (the "Series A Preferred"), convertible into common stock at the option of the holder.

1.4 Use of proceeds. The proceeds will fund product development, go-to-market expansion, customer success capacity and general working capital, in line with the operating plan the Company has shared with Halvard during diligence. Proceeds will not be used to repurchase shares or to repay founder loans, except as approved by the Board.

1.5 Option pool. Before closing, the Company's option pool will be expanded to 12% of the post-money capitalization. The expansion is included in the pre-money valuation, so that it dilutes existing holders and not the Series A investors. Unallocated options in the pool at closing count toward the 12%.

PART 2. ECONOMIC TERMS OF THE SERIES A PREFERRED

2.1 Liquidation preference. One times the original purchase price, non-participating, payable ahead of the common stock and any junior series. In a liquidation or deemed liquidation event, holders of Series A Preferred will receive the greater of (a) the liquidation preference, plus any declared but unpaid dividends, or (b) the amount they would receive had they converted to common stock.

2.2 Dividends. Non-cumulative, payable only when, as and if declared by the Board. No dividends may be paid on the common stock unless the same dividend is paid on an as-converted basis on the Series A Preferred.

2.3 Conversion. Each share of Series A Preferred will be convertible at any time into common stock at an initial one-to-one ratio, and will convert automatically on a qualified public offering or with the consent of the holders of a majority of the Series A Preferred.

2.4 Anti-dilution. Broad-based weighted-average protection on the conversion price, with customary carve-outs for shares issued under the option pool, on conversion of existing securities, and in approved acquisitions.

2.5 Pro rata rights. Each investor holding Series A Preferred may participate in future equity financings in proportion to its ownership, on customary terms.

PART 3. GOVERNANCE

3.1 Board of Directors. Immediately after closing, the Board will have three directors: Maya Lindqvist, Arjun Mehra and Ines Calder (Halvard Partners). Rufus Penn (Gannet Ventures) will serve as a non-voting observer. The observer may attend meetings and receive materials at the same time as directors, subject to confidentiality undertakings and to exclusion from sessions where attendance would risk privilege or present a conflict of interest.

3.2 Board mechanics. The Board will meet at least quarterly. Directors will be reimbursed for reasonable out-of-pocket expenses incurred in attending meetings. Any change to the size of the Board requires the approval of the holders of a majority of the Series A Preferred.

3.3 Protective provisions. For as long as any Series A Preferred remains outstanding, the Company will not, without the written consent of the holders of a majority of the Series A Preferred:

(a) amend the certificate of incorporation or bylaws in a manner that adversely affects the rights of the Series A Preferred;

(b) create or issue any security senior to or on parity with the Series A Preferred;

(c) increase or decrease the authorized number of shares of Series A Preferred, or increase the size of the option pool beyond the level set out in Section 1.5;

(d) declare or pay any dividend or redeem or repurchase any shares, other than repurchases from departing service providers at cost under agreed vesting arrangements;

(e) sell, merge or consolidate the Company, or license all or substantially all of its intellectual property outside the ordinary course of business;

(f) incur indebtedness above a threshold to be set in the definitive agreements; or

(g) liquidate, dissolve or wind up the Company.

3.4 Information rights. Halvard will receive annual financial statements, quarterly unaudited statements, an annual budget approved by the Board, and reasonable inspection rights.

3.5 Other investor rights. Customary right of first refusal and co-sale on transfers by the founders, drag-along rights on a sale approved by the Board and the holders of a majority of the Series A Preferred, and registration rights on customary terms.

PART 4. CLOSING CONDITIONS AND EXPENSES

4.1 Conditions to closing. Closing is subject to:

(a) completion of business, financial, technical, legal and intellectual property due diligence to Halvard's reasonable satisfaction;

(b) negotiation and execution of definitive agreements, including a stock purchase agreement, an amended and restated certificate of incorporation, an investors' rights agreement, a voting agreement and a right of first refusal and co-sale agreement;

(c) approval of the transaction by the Company's Board and stockholders, including the option pool expansion described in Section 1.5;

(d) confirmatory invention assignment and confidentiality agreements from all current employees and contractors who have contributed to the Company's software and models;

(e) delivery of a customary legal opinion of Company counsel and a good standing certificate;

(f) no material adverse change in the business, assets, prospects or financial condition of the Company between the date of this term sheet and closing; and

(g) the Company's capitalization table, as delivered to Halvard, being confirmed as accurate in the definitive agreements.

4.2 Legal fees. Kitefold will reimburse Halvard's legal fees up to $45,000, payable at closing out of the proceeds. If the financing does not close, Halvard bears its own legal fees, except as provided in Section 5.3.

4.3 Documentation. Halvard's counsel will prepare the first drafts of the definitive agreements. The Company's counsel will be responsible for the Company's own legal costs.

PART 5. BINDING PROVISIONS AND GENERAL

5.1 Non-binding nature. Apart from Sections 5.2, 5.3 and 5.4, this term sheet creates no legal obligation on any party.

5.2 Confidentiality. The existence and terms of this term sheet are confidential. The Company may disclose them to its directors, officers, employees, counsel and advisors who need to know, and to Rufus Penn in his capacity as observer, each of whom must be bound to keep them confidential. Halvard will treat all non-public information received in diligence as confidential.

5.3 Exclusivity. After countersigning this term sheet, the Company will not solicit, negotiate or accept any competing offer for an equity investment or a sale of the Company until the earlier of the signing of definitive agreements and written notice by either party that discussions have ended. If the Company breaches this section and then completes an equity financing with another investor, it will reimburse Halvard's reasonable documented legal fees up to the cap in Section 4.2.

5.4 Governing law. This term sheet is governed by the laws of the jurisdiction to be specified in the definitive agreements.

5.5 Counterparts. This term sheet may be signed in counterparts and delivered electronically. It remains open for acceptance until it is countersigned by the Company or withdrawn in writing by Halvard.
