# Kitefold Data Processing Addendum (Edition of April 2026)

Dated April 6, 2026

This Data Processing Addendum (the "Addendum") forms part of the Master Subscription Agreement (the "MSA") between Kitefold, Inc. ("Kitefold," "we," "us") and the customer named in the applicable order form (the "Customer"). It describes how we process personal data when we provide the Kitefold platform, including the editor, hosting and delivery of published experiences, and the analytics that go with them.

1. Scope, Definitions and Precedence

1.1 This Addendum applies to all personal data that Kitefold processes on behalf of the Customer in connection with the MSA. Terms such as "controller," "processor," "personal data," "processing" and "data subject" have the meanings given in applicable data protection law.

1.2 "Viewer" means an individual who opens a published experience on a device. "Viewer Device Data" and "Analytics Events" are described in clause 3.

1.3 If this Addendum conflicts with the MSA on a question of personal data, this Addendum prevails. In every other respect the MSA continues to apply unchanged.

2. Roles of the Parties

2.1 For personal data about Viewers that is collected through experiences the Customer publishes, the Customer is the controller and Kitefold is the processor.

2.2 For account data about the Customer's own users, such as names, work email addresses and sign-in records, Kitefold acts as a controller for the limited purposes of providing, securing and billing for the service, and handles that data under our privacy notice.

2.3 The Customer is responsible for having a lawful basis for collecting Viewer data, for giving Viewers any notices required by law, and for obtaining any consent needed before a published experience accesses a device camera or sensors.

3. Categories of Data and Data Subjects

3.1 Data subjects. Viewers of the Customer's published experiences, and the Customer's authorized users of the Kitefold editor.

3.2 Viewer Device Data. Technical information a device sends when it loads an experience: IP address, browser and operating system type, device model class, screen size, language setting, and the camera and motion-sensor capabilities needed to run the experience. Camera frames used to display an experience are processed on the Viewer's device and are not uploaded to Kitefold.

3.3 Analytics Events. Records generated while an experience runs, such as a load, a session start, an interaction, a tap on a call-to-action, and session length, each linked to a pseudonymous session identifier rather than to a name or email address.

3.4 Kitefold does not ask the Customer to send us special categories of personal data, and the Customer agrees not to configure an experience to collect them.

4. Processing Instructions

4.1 Kitefold processes personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions, which consist of the MSA, this Addendum, the order form, and the Customer's configuration of the platform.

4.2 The purposes of processing are hosting and delivering published experiences, measuring their performance for the Customer, keeping the service secure and available, and providing support the Customer requests.

4.3 We do not sell personal data, and we do not use Viewer data for advertising or to build profiles for our own purposes. If we believe an instruction breaks the law, we will tell the Customer and may pause that instruction until it is clarified.

5. Confidentiality and Personnel

5.1 Everyone at Kitefold who can reach Customer personal data is bound by written confidentiality obligations and receives data protection training as part of onboarding and on a recurring basis.

5.2 Access is granted on a need-to-know basis, tied to the person's role, and removed promptly when the role changes or the person leaves the company.

6. Security Measures

6.1 Kitefold maintains technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risk, including:

(a) encryption of personal data in transit between devices, our platform and our subprocessors, and encryption at rest for stored data;

(b) role-based access control with individual accounts, strong authentication for administrative access, and logging of access to production systems;

(c) separation of customer environments and of production from development and testing, with no production personal data used for testing;

(d) a documented process for reviewing and applying security updates to our systems and dependencies;

(e) backups and a tested recovery procedure for the systems that hold Customer data; and

(f) written incident response procedures, reviewed after any significant event.

6.2 Kitefold may update these measures as the service evolves, provided that updates do not materially reduce the overall level of protection.

6.3 IP address minimization. IP addresses are truncated after 24 hours. Until truncation they are used only to deliver the experience, to protect the service against abuse, and to derive coarse location at country or region level for analytics.

7. Subprocessors

7.1 The Customer gives general authorization for Kitefold to use the following subprocessors: Driftnet Cloud (hosting) and Swiftline Edge (content delivery).

7.2 Kitefold has a written agreement with each subprocessor that imposes data protection obligations no less protective than those in this Addendum. Kitefold remains responsible to the Customer for its subprocessors' performance of those obligations.

7.3 Customers get 30 days' notice before a new subprocessor is added. We give that notice in writing to the Customer's account owner and at the email address designated for legal notices in the order form.

7.4 During the notice period the Customer may object in writing on reasonable data protection grounds. The parties will then work in good faith to find a solution. If none is found, the Customer may end the affected part of the service under the MSA and receive a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees for the unused portion.

8. Personal Data Breach

8.1 Kitefold notifies customers of a personal data breach within 48 hours of becoming aware of it.

8.2 The notice will describe, as far as known at that point, the nature of the breach, the categories of data and approximate number of Viewers affected, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed. Where all details are not yet available, we will provide them in stages as our investigation continues.

8.3 Kitefold will take reasonable steps to contain the breach, investigate its cause, and prevent a recurrence, and will cooperate with the Customer's own notifications to regulators and affected individuals. Decisions about notifying regulators or Viewers rest with the Customer as controller.

8.4 Our notice of a breach is not an admission of fault or liability.

9. Data Subject Requests and Assistance

9.1 If a Viewer contacts Kitefold directly with a request relating to data we process for the Customer, we will direct the Viewer to the Customer and will not respond on the substance unless the law requires it.

9.2 Kitefold will give the Customer reasonable help, using the platform's export and deletion tools where possible, so the Customer can respond to access, correction, deletion, restriction and objection requests.

9.3 On request, Kitefold will reasonably assist the Customer with data protection impact assessments and consultations with regulators that relate to the processing described here.

10. Retention

10.1 Raw analytics events are kept for 13 months. After that period they are deleted or reduced to aggregated statistics that no longer relate to an identifiable Viewer.

10.2 IP addresses are truncated after 24 hours, as described in clause 6.3. The truncated value is kept only as long as the analytics event it belongs to.

10.3 Viewer Device Data held in delivery and security logs is kept only as long as needed for troubleshooting and abuse prevention, and then deleted on a rolling basis.

10.4 Content the Customer uploads, such as 3D models, images and project files, is kept while the Customer's account is active, unless the Customer deletes it sooner.

11. Deletion and Return of Data

11.1 When the MSA ends, the Customer may export its project content and analytics reports through the platform. We will keep the account available for export for a reasonable wind-down period stated in the MSA.

11.2 After the wind-down period Kitefold deletes the Customer's personal data from active systems, and from backups as they cycle out in the ordinary course, unless the law requires us to keep it. Data kept for that reason stays protected under this Addendum and is not used for any other purpose.

11.3 On written request, Kitefold will confirm in writing that deletion has been completed.

11.4 The Customer may also ask us to delete specific data during the term, for example to honor a Viewer's request, and we will do so within a reasonable time.

12. Audits, Term and Contact

12.1 Kitefold will make available the information reasonably necessary to show compliance with this Addendum, such as summaries of our security practices and answers to the Customer's written security questionnaires.

12.2 If that information does not reasonably answer the Customer's concerns, or if a regulator requires it, the Customer may carry out an audit, no more than once in any twelve-month period unless a breach has occurred. The Customer will give reasonable written notice, agree the scope and timing with us in advance, keep audit findings confidential, avoid disrupting our operations or exposing other customers' data, and bear its own costs.

12.3 This Addendum takes effect on April 6, 2026 for new order forms and remains in force for as long as Kitefold processes personal data on the Customer's behalf under the MSA.

12.4 Questions about this Addendum, requests under clauses 7 to 11 and legal notices may be sent to the privacy contact listed in the Kitefold account settings.
