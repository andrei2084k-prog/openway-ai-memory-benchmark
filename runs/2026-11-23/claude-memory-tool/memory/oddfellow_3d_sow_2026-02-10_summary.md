# Oddfellow 3D Studio <-> Kitefold SOW (dated 2026-02-10) - key terms
(User attached full PDF in chat; user will say what they need next. Used for planning client-experience content.)
Parties: Oddfellow (Marta Oddny, studio lead) / Kitefold (Maya Lindqvist, founder+CEO, authorized approver). Signature lines blank in the copy I saw.

## Tiers (confirmed in writing per model before work starts, 1.2)
- S simple (bottle, mug, box, signage): low poly, 1 material, 1 texture set (base color only). $180, 5 biz days
- M standard (sneaker, small appliance, furniture): medium poly, up to 3 materials, up to 2 texture sets (base, roughness, metalness, normal). $420, 8 biz days
- L complex (hero objects, many parts, fine geometry): high poly, baked detail, up to 4 texture sets (emissive/transparency where needed), plus lower-detail version for slow devices. $950, 12 biz days
- Exact poly/texture limits are in a tech spec sheet sent at kickoff (part of SOW) - NOT in the doc. 2.4 has typo "Marta-to-Kitefold".
- Tier change: Oddfellow must flag before starting; Kitefold can approve, adjust brief, or cancel that model free (2.5).

## Prices: USD, ex sales tax. Cover modeling, texturing, optimization, 2 revision rounds, delivery. No PM/file prep fee.

## Turnaround
- Business days from Oddfellow confirming complete brief + all reference. = first complete version delivered.
- Incomplete reference: Oddfellow flags within 1 biz day; clock starts when missing items arrive.
- Batches: scheduled in Kitefold's ranked order; combined schedule at kickoff.

## Revisions
- 2 rounds included; round = one consolidated message of changes within approved brief.
- Out of scope (new colorway, different object, added parts, tier change) = quoted separately, approved before work.
- Revision return: 2 biz days (S), 3 biz days (M, L). Extra rounds quoted in writing first.

## Delivery
- .glb primary (textures embedded). USDZ free on request. Separate texture images on request. Editable source scene on request.
- Private download link to Kitefold's named contact; active until receipt confirmed.

## Acceptance / payment
- Kitefold has 5 biz days to accept or send revisions. No response -> 1 reminder -> 2 biz days later deemed accepted.
- Acceptance doesn't remove duty to fix load/display defects.
- Invoice per model on acceptance; net 30 from invoice date; to billing contact named at kickoff.
- Cancel after start, before delivery: Oddfellow may invoice work done, capped at tier price.

## Rights / term
- Rights in model + textures transfer on payment of that model's invoice (excl. Oddfellow tools/techniques/pre-existing assets).
- Confidential; Oddfellow can't publicly show Kitefold work without written permission.
- Term: from both-signed until 30 days' written notice; in-progress/accepted models completed and paid.

## Planning math shortcuts
- Timeline for one model ~ turnaround + up to 5 review days + revision rounds (2-3 days each) -> e.g. Tier M with 2 revisions ≈ 8 + 5 + 3 + 3 biz days worst-ish case.
